شروط الشهادة

Shuroot ash-Shahaadah

The Conditions of “Lā ilāha illā Allāh”

Open Open Tab Download

Pages:

Topic: Aqeedah – Conditions of the Shahadah

Level: Beginner

What you will learn:

The well-known conditions of the statement “Lā ilāha illā Allāh”: knowledge, certainty, acceptance, submission, truthfulness, sincerity and love – along with evidences from the Qur’an and Sunnah and how these conditions appear in the speech and practice of the Salaf.

About this booklet:

A concise Salafi treatment of the conditions of the Shahadah, clarifying that simply uttering the words is not enough without belief, knowledge and compliance, and warning against common misunderstandings which weaken a person’s Tawheed and allegiance (walā’) to Allah and His religion.

مقدمة الرسالة

Muqaddimat ar-Risālah

Creed of Ibn Abī Zayd al-Qayrawānī

Open Open Tab Download

Pages:

Topic: Classical Salafi Creed Text

Level: Beginner–Intermediate

What you will learn:

A summary of the creed of Ahlus-Sunnah wal-Jamā‘ah in the words of the Māliki Imam Ibn Abī Zayd: belief in Allah’s Names and Attributes without distortion, the Qur’an as the speech of Allah, the reality of Qadar, the status of the Companions, and the importance of following the Sunnah and avoiding innovation.

About the book & author:

Imām Abū Muhammad ‘Abdullāh ibn Abī Zayd al-Qayrawānī (d. 386H) was known as “the little Mālik” due to his deep knowledge of the madhhab. His famous ar-Risālah begins with this short Aqeedah section, which became a reference in clarifying the creed of the early Salaf in North Africa and beyond.

أصول الإسلام

Usul al-Islam

Foundations of the Religion

Open Open Tab Download

Pages:

Topic: Foundations of Islam & Imaan

Level: Beginner–Intermediate

What you will learn:

The basic principles of Islam: the testimony of faith, the pillars of Islam and Imaan, obedience to Allah and His Messenger, and the necessity of following the path of the believers and avoiding the ways of shirk, hypocrisy and innovation.

About the book & author:

This treatise is attributed to Imām Muhammad ibn ‘Abd al-Wahhāb (rahimahullah), the well-known scholar of Najd and caller to Tawheed. It summarises the core matters a Muslim must know and act upon, in the same spirit as his other short works such as Thalāthat al-Usool, al-Qawā‘id al-Arba‘ and al-Usool as-Sittah.
