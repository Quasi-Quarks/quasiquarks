📚 Library

Read authentic books on Tawheed, Aqeedah and Fiqh.

The library area is organised into simple sections so you don’t feel lost. You can open each PDF inside the website, or in a new tab, or download it.

  • Introduction: basic texts in creed and belief for new learners.
  • Tawheed: classic books like Kitab at-Tawheed and their explanations.
  • Aqeedah: core Salafi creed texts and commentaries.
  • Fiqh: worship, prayer, fasting, Hajj, marriage and more.
Introduction section ↗ Tawheed books Aqeedah library Fiqh & worship

You can always come back to this homepage using your browser’s back button or by adding a simple “Home” link in each section later.

Tip: Recommended path

If you’re completely new, you can follow this order:

  • 1. Introduction – basics of faith and creed.
  • 2. Tawheed – understanding worship of Allah alone.
  • 3. Aqeedah – detailed belief of Ahlus-Sunnah.
  • 4. Fiqh – how to pray, fast, etc. correctly.

🌙 Prophets’ Stories

Learn about the lives of the prophets, from Adam to Muhammad ﷺ.

Many people connect with Islam by hearing the stories of the prophets: their struggles, patience, du‘ā, miracles, families, and how Allah supported them.

Here you can link to playlists that tell these stories from authentic sources – not fairy tales – so that the lessons are correct and beneficial.

Watch Prophets’ stories ↗ (Future) Prophets’ stories page

Later you can also connect each story to a PDF in your library that explains the ‘aqeedah side, like Nūh’s call to Tawheed or Ibrāhīm’s da‘wah against shirk.

Suggested prophets to highlight
  • Ādam – the first human and prophet.
  • Nūh – calling to Tawheed for 950 years.
  • Ibrāhīm – the father of the prophets, pure Tawheed.
  • Mūsā – confronting oppression with faith and patience.
  • ‘Īsā – the true story of Jesus in Islam.
  • Muhammad ﷺ – the final messenger for all mankind.
