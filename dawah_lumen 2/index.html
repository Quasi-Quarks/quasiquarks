كتاب التوحيد

Kitab at-Tawheed

The Book of Islamic Monotheism

Open Open Tab Download

Pages: ~130 (varies by edition)

Topic: Tawheed (Islamic Monotheism)

Level: Core / Beginner–Intermediate

What you will learn:

The foundations of Tawheed as explained by the Imams of the Salaf: singling out Allah in worship, understanding major and minor shirk, ruling on graves, amulets and intercession, and how Quranic ayat and authentic ahadith clarify the nullifiers of Tawheed.

About the book & author:

Authored by Imam Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab (rahimahullah), a scholar of Najd upon the creed of the Salaf, this classic text gathers evidences on Tawheed al-Uluhiyyah, ar-Ruboobiyyah and al-Asma wa’s-Sifaat, refuting practices of grave-worship, du‘a to the dead and other forms of shirk that contradict pure worship of Allah alone.

شرح كتاب التوحيد

Explanation of Kitab at-Tawheed

A Detailed Salafi Commentary on Tawheed

Open Open Tab Download

Pages: ~600 (commentary, varies by print)

Topic: Detailed Explanation of Tawheed

Level: Intermediate–Advanced

What you will learn:

A deeper, chapter-by-chapter explanation of the issues raised in Kitab at-Tawheed: categories of worship, the meaning of la ilaha illa Allah, refutation of modern doubts, understanding riyaa’, taweeq (amulets), seeking help from the dead, and practical ways to protect one’s Tawheed in everyday life.

About the commentary:

This work presents a classical Salafi explanation of Imam Ibn Abd al-Wahhab’s text, relying on Qur’an, authentic Sunnah and the statements of the early scholars. It clarifies difficult phrases, expands on key issues of ‘aqeedah and warns against the types of shirk and innovations that entered into the Ummah after the best generations.

كتاب التوحيد

Kitab at-Tawheed (Matn)

Core Text of Tawheed with Translation

Open Open Tab Download

Pages: ~130 (matn + translation)

Topic: Core Text in Tawheed

Level: Core / Beginner–Intermediate

What you will learn:

The concise chapters of the original Book of Tawheed, each built around Qur’anic ayat and sahih ahadith. You will see how the Salaf understood Tawheed practically: in du‘a, fear, hope, love, slaughtering, vows, seeking barakah and seeking help – all directed only to Allah.

About the book & author:

This is another edition of the famous matn of Imam Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab (rahimahullah). His writings, as explained by major scholars like Ibn Baz, Ibn ‘Uthaymeen, al-Albani, as-Sa‘di and others, form a central reference in Salafi ‘aqeedah and da‘wah, calling Muslims back to pure worship of Allah alone.

شرح كتاب التوحيد

Sharh Kitab at-Tawheed

Explanation by Imam ‘Abd al-Rahman as-Sa‘di

Open Open Tab Download

Pages: ~280–300 (varies by file/print)

Topic: Detailed Salafi Commentary

Level: Intermediate

What you will learn:

A clear, easy-to-read explanation by Imam as-Sa‘di on every chapter of Kitab at-Tawheed: definitions, benefits, fiqh rulings of Tawheed, refutation of common doubts, and how Tawheed connects to qalb (heart), manners and daily actions of the Muslim.

About the book & author:

Imam ‘Abd al-Rahman ibn Nasir as-Sa‘di (rahimahullah) was a well-known Salafi scholar, teacher of Shaykh Ibn ‘Uthaymeen, and author of the tafsir Taysir al-Karim ar-Rahman. His books are characterised by clarity, strong evidence and gentle style, making this one of the most accessible explanations of Kitab at-Tawheed.

القول المفيد على كتاب التوحيد

Sharh Kitab at-Tawheed

Detailed Explanation by Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan

Open Open Tab Download

Pages: ~550–600 (multi-volume in print)

Topic: Detailed Lesson-Style Explanation

Level: Advanced Student

What you will learn:

A thorough, lesson-based explanation of every chapter: definition of Tawheed, types of shirk, categories of people regarding Tawheed, the connection between Tawheed and al-Qadr, and many contemporary examples of shirk and superstition. Ideal for serious study circles and note-taking.

About the book & author:

This explanation compiles the classes and writings of Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan (hafizhahullah) on Kitab at-Tawheed. His approach is firmly Salafi, emphasising adherence to Qur’an and Sunnah on the understanding of the Sahabah, and warning strongly against all forms of ghuluw (exaggeration) and shirk in the Ummah.
