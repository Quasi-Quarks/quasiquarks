What you will learn:

A structured, modern commentary on Kitab at-Tawheed: each chapter is explained with simple language, practical examples and clear rulings. You’ll understand how issues like supplicating to graves, seeking blessings from trees, charms, fortunetelling and sacrificing for other than Allah all oppose Tawheed.

About the book & author:

Written by Shaykh Dr Salih al-Fawzan (hafizhahullah), a senior scholar of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, member of the Council of Senior Scholars, known for his strong Salafi ‘aqeedah and clear refutations of shirk and bid‘ah. This commentary is widely used in institutes and masajid that teach Salafi ‘aqeedah.
