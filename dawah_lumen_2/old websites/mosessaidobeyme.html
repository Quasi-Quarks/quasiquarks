Moses’ “Obey Me” Verses in Judaism and Christianity

January 14, 2025 • 5 min read • By Lorem Ipsum

Below is a clean, accurate list of the strongest “Obey me / Hear me / Follow my commands” statements attributed to Moses, from Judaism and Christianity, separated into: (1) Hebrew Bible / Tanakh (Judaism), (2) Old Testament (Christian Bibles), and (3) New Testament references about Moses’ authority. These are the top, clearest verses where Moses commands obedience.

Text size:
1. Judaism (Tanakh / Hebrew Bible) — Moses Saying “Obey Me / My Commands”

These verses are exactly the same as the Christian Old Testament, just arranged differently. These are the strongest “obey me” statements directly from Moses’ mouth.

1. Exodus 15:26

Exodus 15:26
“If you will diligently listen to the voice of YHWH your God and do what is right in His eyes, and give ear to His commandments, and keep all His statutes, I will put none of the diseases on you which I have brought on the Egyptians.”

➡️ Moses commands Israel to OBEY.

2. Exodus 19:5

Exodus 19:5
“Now therefore, if you will indeed obey My voice and keep My covenant, then you shall be My treasured possession…”

Moses relays this command.

3. Exodus 24:7

Exodus 24:7
“We will do and obey all that YHWH has spoken.”

This is Moses presenting the covenant book and Israel promising obedience.

4. Deuteronomy 4:1

Deuteronomy 4:1
“Now, O Israel, listen to the statutes and to the judgments which I teach you to do…”

➡️ Moses explicitly says: listen to what I teach.

5. Deuteronomy 5:1

Deuteronomy 5:1
“Hear, O Israel, the statutes and judgments which I speak in your hearing today, that you may learn them and keep them and do them.”

6. Deuteronomy 6:1–2

Deuteronomy 6:1–2
“These are the commandments… which YHWH your God commanded me to teach you, that you might do them…”

➡️ Moses is commanding obedience to what he teaches.

7. Deuteronomy 11:27

Deuteronomy 11:27
“The blessing, if you obey the commandments of YHWH your God which I command you this day.”

8. Deuteronomy 13:4

Deuteronomy 13:4
“You shall walk after YHWH your God… keep His commandments, obey His voice, serve Him, and cling to Him.”

Moses speaking.

9. Deuteronomy 27:10

Deuteronomy 27:10
“You shall obey the voice of YHWH your God, and do His commandments and His statutes which I command you today.”

10. Deuteronomy 30:11–12

Deuteronomy 30:11–12
“This commandment which I command you today is not too hard for you…”

These are the top 10 clearest direct obedience commands spoken by Moses in Judaism.

⸻
2. Christianity — Old Testament (Moses’ Direct Commands)

Christian Bibles have the same Book of Moses, so the same verses apply. But here are the top 10, chosen specifically for Christian theological relevance:

1. Deuteronomy 4:1

Deuteronomy 4:1
“Listen to the statutes and judgments which I teach you.”

2. Deuteronomy 5:1

Deuteronomy 5:1
“Hear, O Israel, the statutes and judgments which I speak in your hearing.”

3. Deuteronomy 6:1–2

Deuteronomy 6:1–2
“God commanded me to teach you… that you may keep all His statutes.”

4. Deuteronomy 11:27–28

Deuteronomy 11:27–28
“A blessing if you obey… a curse if you do not obey the commandments which I command you.”

5. Deuteronomy 12:32

Deuteronomy 12:32
“Whatever I command you, be careful to observe it; you shall not add to it nor take from it.”

6. Deuteronomy 26:16

Deuteronomy 26:16
“This day YHWH commands you to do these statutes… therefore you shall keep and do them with all your heart.”

7. Deuteronomy 27:10

Deuteronomy 27:10
“You shall obey… the commandments and statutes which I command you today.”

8. Deuteronomy 30:15–16

Deuteronomy 30:15–16
“I command you today to love YHWH your God, to walk in His ways, and keep His commandments.”

9. Deuteronomy 30:19–20

Deuteronomy 30:19–20
“I call heaven and earth to witness… therefore choose life.”

10. Exodus 24:3

Exodus 24:3
“Moses came and told the people all the words of YHWH… and the people said, ‘All the words which YHWH has said we will do.’”

⸻
3. Christianity — New Testament References to Obeying Moses

Moses does not speak directly in the New Testament, but the New Testament commands obedience to Moses or acknowledges that people were supposed to obey him.

1. Matthew 23:2–3

Matthew 23:2–3
“The scribes and Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat. Therefore whatever they tell you to observe, that observe and do.”

➡️ Jesus commands obedience to Moses’ authority.

2. John 5:46–47

John 5:46–47
“If you believed Moses, you would believe Me… but if you do not believe his writings, how will you believe My words?”

➡️ Obeying Moses = obeying Jesus.

3. Acts 3:22

Acts 3:22
“Moses said: ‘The Lord your God will raise up for you a Prophet like me… you must listen to Him in everything.’”

This quotes Moses commanding obedience to the coming Prophet.

4. Acts 15:21

Acts 15:21
“For Moses has been preached… and is read in the synagogues every Sabbath.”

Implying his laws are to be obeyed.

5. Acts 26:22

Acts 26:22
Paul testifies that he is preaching “nothing except what Moses and the Prophets said” — affirming obedience.

6. Hebrews 10:28

Hebrews 10:28
“Anyone who rejected Moses’ law died without mercy…”

➡️ You were expected to obey Moses.
